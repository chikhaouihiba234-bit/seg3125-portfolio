How I Work

Still figuring it out, but here's how I tend to approach things.

01
Research & Understand

I start by looking at what already exists and figuring out what's actually wrong with it. Sites like the Nielsen Norman Group have been useful for understanding the reasoning behind good design.

02
Sketch & Structure

Before writing any code, I try to sketch a rough layout on paper or in Figma. Getting the structure right early saves a lot of back-and-forth later. I apply the same logic with other tools I use at work, like Power Apps and Power Automate — structure first, build second.

03
Build & Refine

Whether it's HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap 5 or a flow in Power Automate, I build incrementally and adjust based on how it actually works in practice. I'm taking SEG3125 at uOttawa mostly to put proper names to things I'd already been bumping into at work.

Helpful resources I've been using: Nielsen Norman Group, Interaction Design Foundation, the Bootstrap 5 docs, and FreeCodeCamp (currently working through their full stack developer path).
